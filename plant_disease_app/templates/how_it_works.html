How CropScan Works

A complete walkthrough of the AI pipeline — from photo upload to disease diagnosis.

The Detection Pipeline

Every image you upload goes through these 5 steps in under 3 seconds.

1

📷 You Upload a Leaf Photo

You drag & drop or select a JPG/PNG photo of a crop leaf. The browser previews it instantly. Only image files are accepted — the app validates the format before sending.

HTML File APIJavaScriptFlask Upload
2

💾 Flask Saves the Image Securely

The Python Flask server receives the image, generates a unique random filename (UUID) so no two files ever clash, and saves it to the static/uploads/ folder on your computer. It never leaves your machine.

Python FlaskUUIDWerkzeug
3

🔬 Image is Preprocessed for the AI

The image is resized to exactly 224 × 224 pixels — the input size MobileNetV2 requires. Pixel values are normalised from 0–255 to the range the model was trained on using TensorFlow's preprocess_input().

TensorFlowKerasNumPy
4

🧠 MobileNetV2 Analyses the Image

The preprocessed image is passed through MobileNetV2 — a deep convolutional neural network with 53 layers. It extracts features like colour patterns, texture, spot shapes, and lesion edges. The model outputs a probability score for each of its classes, and the highest score is selected as the prediction.

MobileNetV2CNNImageNet Weights
5
✓

📋 Results Displayed with Treatment Plan

The predicted class is mapped to one of 38 PlantVillage disease labels. The app then looks up the full disease profile from its database and renders the results page — showing the disease name, confidence score, symptoms, causes, treatment recommendations, and prevention tips.

Jinja2 TemplatesDisease DatabaseHTML/CSS

What It Can & Can't Do

Be aware of the current limitations before relying on results.

✅ What Works Well

  • Detects 38 disease conditions
  • Works across 14 crop types
  • Runs fully offline on your PC
  • Results in under 3 seconds
  • Provides treatment & prevention info
  • No internet required after setup

⚠️ Current Limitations

  • Not fine-tuned on PlantVillage dataset yet
  • Best with clear close-up leaf photos
  • May struggle with blurry images
  • Not a replacement for an agronomist
  • Confidence scores are approximate
  • Works best with tomato/common crops

How to Make It More Accurate

The app is designed so you can plug in a properly trained model in minutes.

🔧 Upgrade Path

Replace the predict_disease() function in app.py with a model trained on the PlantVillage dataset (54,000+ images, freely available on Kaggle). Train a MobileNetV2 with transfer learning — fine-tune the last few layers on the 38-class PlantVillage data — and you'll get 95%+ accuracy. The rest of the app needs zero changes.
